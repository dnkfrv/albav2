Alba Bistro – brunch, specialty coffee & matcha bar in Lisbon

Alba Bistro is a brunch café at Largo do Rato in Lisbon with a sunny terrace on the square. We serve breakfast and brunch every day, specialty coffee and a dedicated ceremonial matcha bar.

Our brunch goes beyond avocado toast: omelettes, savory and sweet crêpes, eggs Benedict with salmon or pastrami, croque madame and creamy oatmeal. On weekdays we also serve lunch from 12:00.

The matcha bar pours ceremonial matcha by Michi – Ikigai, Yutori, Haru and Hojicha – as shots, lattes and signature drinks, plus a tasting set of three varieties. For coffee: espresso, batch brew, hand brew, cold brew and signature lattes.
