Alba – brunch, specialty coffee and matcha bar in Lisbon

Alba is a brunch café, specialty coffee and matcha bar on Largo do Rato, a short walk from Príncipe Real. Two years and counting. Time Out calls our terrace one of the best – and most unlikely – in Lisbon. We agree on both counts.

Yes, there's avocado toast. There's also a four-cheese omelette with onion jam, crêpe Suzette and savory oatmeal with a poached egg. Brunch every day 9:00–17:00, lunch on weekdays 13:00–15:00. Vegan option and oat milk included.

The coffee is specialty, the matcha is ceremonial by Michi – as a shot, a latte or a tasting set of three. Beans from our roasters are on the shelf to take home. Come as you are.
